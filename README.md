## CTF Highlights
During the NCL Fall 2026 CTF I gained experience learning all aspects of cybersecurity with a hands-on approach. I especially enjoyed password cracking, log analysis, forensics, and OSINT activities. 

I gained experience with many tools throughout the CTF including reverse engineering with Ghidra, analyzing network traffic using Wireshark, mapping networks and pivoting using Nmap and Netcat, cracking hashes with Hashcat, exploiting code flaws with Python, analyzing logs with commonly used CLI tools, and so much more.

I also gained experience leading a team by volunteering as team captain during the team game. My team was full of amazing and talented people, and together we were able to get 46th place out of over 3000 teams on the NCL team game with 100% completion putting us in the 99th percentile!

Participating in the CTF has inspired me to adapt online CTFs such as OverTheWire:Wargames, Hack The Box, and TryHackMe as an efficient way to learn security concepts with a hands-on approach.

Cryptography and pivoting were the hardest categories for me. Before the next CTF, I plan to review my notes, practice those areas on platforms like OverTheWire and TryHackMe, and get faster with the tools I used.
